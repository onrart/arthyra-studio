Arthyra Studio / Güne Bir Söz

Privacy Policy

How information stays on your device, what happens when the app connects to the internet, and how you can manage your records.

Last updated: 4 November 2026Android · com.onrart.gunlukalinti

At a glance: Favorites and preferences are stored on your device. Refreshing quotations connects to Wikiquote. Reminders are local, and sharing is initiated by you.

1. Scope and publisher

This policy explains data handling in Güne Bir Söz, the Android app published by Arthyra Studio. The app provides daily quotations, favorites, optional local reminders, a home screen widget and sharing.

The app does not require an account or ask you to enter a name, phone number or email address. It does not use advertisements or behavioral analytics tools. This description reflects the features of version 1.1.1.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores favorite quotation identifiers, reminder preferences and time, downloaded quotations and source information, and selected daily quotations in its local app storage. Daily selections keep the same quotation consistent across the app, reminders and widget.

These records are not uploaded to an Arthyra Studio user server. The widget receives the quotations it displays from the app on your device.

3. Internet connections and third parties

At startup, on returning to the foreground, or when you manually refresh the collection, the app may request public author pages from the Turkish Wikiquote API. Automatic refreshes are subject to a waiting interval. Your favorites and reminder preferences are not included in these requests.

As with any internet connection, Wikimedia can receive your IP address and technical request information needed to provide its service. Its processing is governed by the Wikimedia Privacy Policy. Opening a source link connects your browser to the relevant website.

Downloading and updating the app through Google Play are platform activities governed by Google’s own terms and privacy policy.

4. Reminders and permissions

Reminders are scheduled on your device using Android’s local notification system. The app does not register you for a remote push notification service or request a push token to send these reminders.

The notification permission, and exact alarm permission where supported, are used to provide reminders at your chosen time. You can disable notifications in the app or Android settings.

5. Sharing and generated images

When you choose to share, the selected quotation text or an image generated from it is passed to the Android share sheet. You select the destination app or recipient. The app does not share automatically.

Story images are created on your device and may be kept in temporary app files. They are not automatically uploaded to Arthyra Studio. A destination app or service you select may process the shared content under its own privacy policy.

6. Retention, deletion and device backups

Favorites and preferences may remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app. You can remove an individual favorite within the app. Android’s app storage/data clearing options reset favorites, preferences and downloaded quotations.

System backups: Depending on your Android version, device and backup settings, copies of app data may be included in system backups and restored after reinstallation. Arthyra Studio does not operate these backups. Manage them through Android or your backup provider’s settings. Android backup information.

Temporary sharing images may be removed by cache management or clearing app data. There is no in-app user account to close.

7. Security and limits

Online quotation requests use HTTPS. Local records are stored in the app storage area on your device. Your device lock, software updates, operating system permissions and chosen backup service also affect their security.

The app does not offer features based on contacts, microphone, camera or precise location access. We do not use your data for ad targeting or sell it.

8. Children’s privacy

The app does not ask for your age or date of birth. We do not request children’s personal information. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us through a support message, please contact us below.

9. This website and policy updates

This website does not include advertisements, visitor analytics or tracking cookies added by us. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information under its own service terms. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

We update this policy when app features or data handling changes. The current policy and its last updated date are published on this page.
